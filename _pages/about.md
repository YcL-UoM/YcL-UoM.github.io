I am a **Ph.D. candidate in Operations Research and Scientific Computing** at the University of Michigan, advised by [Prof. Ruiwei Jiang](https://ioe.engin.umich.edu/people/jiang-ruiwei/). I am also pursuing a dual master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering. Before joining the University of Michigan, I received my B.S. in Industrial Engineering from Tsinghua University.

I am interested in using mathematical modeling and optimization tools to solve complex, large-scale real-world problems. My current research focuses on **decision-making under uncertainty**, with applications to power systems. I develop stochastic and robust optimization models, mixed-integer formulations, and scalable decomposition algorithms to connect day-ahead planning with real-time operations.

Through a student fellowship at **Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL)**, I collaborate with [Alexandre Moreira da Silva](https://eta.lbl.gov/people/alexandre-moreira-da-silva) on storage participation in sequential electricity markets.

## Research Interests

- **Mathematical optimization:** modeling and solving complex, large-scale decision problems.
- **Decision-making under uncertainty:** developing methods for reliable and effective decisions in uncertain environments.
- **Machine learning and data-driven methods:** integrating data, learning, and optimization to address real-world challenges.
